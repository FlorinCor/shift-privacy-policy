SHIFT

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 1 November 2026

SHIFT is an Android puzzle game provided by Cordunianu Florin. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

A free game for families. SHIFT includes Google AdMob banner advertising, with an age-group choice and privacy controls. The family version does not display full-screen app-open ads at startup, including for adults. Development APKs use Google's test ad units; Google's advertising and privacy services can still process data in those builds.

Game data on your device

SHIFT stores puzzle progress, completion marks, the current piece position and game preferences in the app's private storage so you can continue playing. A coarse age-group choice, advertising privacy choices and basic startup state are also stored locally. The game does not require an account, and the game does not send those saved puzzle states to the developer. SHIFT does not operate a game-account or cloud-save service and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

You can clear normal-game progress through Settings → Reset progress. This does not reset your age group or advertising privacy choices. Android's app-storage controls can remove all local app data. Android may include app data in device backups according to your device and account settings; the developer does not receive those backups. Resetting local progress does not delete copies managed by Android backup services or data independently held by an advertising provider.

Age groups and children

Before setting up advertising, SHIFT asks you to choose Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say. No birth date or identity document is requested. The selected group is saved on your device; the SDK receives the corresponding child, teen or adult treatment settings rather than an exact birth date.

Under-13 players, ages 13–15, and players of unknown age receive Google's child-protective ad treatment. Ages 16–17 receive teen treatment. All groups use a maximum ad-content rating of G. Child and unknown-age requests are configured to restrict interest-based advertising and remarketing. SHIFT bundles Google's Families self-certified Mobile Ads SDK and no additional mediation SDKs.

You can change the saved group through Settings → Age group (or the Age group control on the level screen in benchmark builds). Changing it discards the currently displayed ad before advertising is configured for the new group. The game remains playable when advertising cannot be authorized or loaded. A parent or guardian can contact the developer about a child's privacy or request deletion of correspondence sent to the developer.

Google advertising and privacy services

Google's services can process an IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers where available and permitted. These support ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing varies with age treatment, platform and privacy choices.

SHIFT removes Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions from its manifest and disables Google's publisher first-party ID. This is not a claim that every identifier is unavailable on every Android version: Google's age-protective settings and your privacy choices also govern SDK handling. The game does not send the developer your puzzle solution or saved progress.

This processing is described by Google's privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google describes SDK transmissions as encrypted in transit. Google and its providers may process data in countries other than yours, under the safeguards described in its policy. Google and its service or advertising providers handle advertising information under their applicable policies.

Advertising privacy choices

Google's User Messaging Platform checks the privacy requirements for the selected age treatment and location. Its consent/privacy service connects to Google to obtain that information even when an ad is not shown. Where required, the app presents Google's privacy form before initializing or requesting advertising. Advertising proceeds only when Google's privacy status permits it; a missing decision or setup failure is not treated as permission.

When applicable, Settings → Ad privacy choices, or the equivalent control on the level screen in benchmark builds, lets you revisit Google's privacy form. The entry also allows a retry when privacy setup is unavailable. Under-13, 13–15 and unknown-age players are marked as under the age of consent for Google's privacy service. Older teens may still receive a privacy form while teen ad treatment applies. You can additionally manage available advertising controls in Android settings. Less personalized advertising can still require limited processing for ad delivery and fraud prevention. Clearing game progress alone does not withdraw advertising choices or delete information already held by Google.

When you contact the developer

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include. This information is used to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. Correspondence is kept only as needed for these purposes and any applicable legal obligations; you may request its deletion by email.

Sharing, retention and security

The developer does not sell your support correspondence or saved game progress. Email services process correspondence to deliver and store messages. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Advertising and consent data is processed by Google as explained above; this is separate from local puzzle progress.

Local game data remains until you reset it, remove app data or otherwise delete it, subject to Android backup settings. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data Google holds; see its retention explanation. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no device, storage or transmission method guarantees complete security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or transfer personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com to request help with information held by the developer. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully performed. For information held independently by Google, use the controls and contact methods in Google's privacy policy.

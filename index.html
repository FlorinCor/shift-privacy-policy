SHIFT

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 1 November 2026

SHIFT is an Android puzzle game provided by Cordunianu Florin. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Current build and planned advertising. SHIFT is free. The current development build has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. An ad-supported release using Google AdMob is planned. The advertising section below explains that planned service; it does not mean the current development build already transmits advertising data. This page will be updated to match the advertising implementation before that release.

Game data on your device

SHIFT stores puzzle progress, completion marks, the current piece position and game preferences in the app's private storage so you can continue playing. It does not require an account, and the game does not send those saved puzzle states to the developer. The current build does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

You can clear normal-game progress through Settings → Reset progress. Android's app-storage controls can remove all local app data. Android may include app data in device backups according to your device and account settings; the developer does not receive those backups. Resetting local progress does not delete copies managed by Android backup services or data independently held by an advertising provider.

Planned Google AdMob advertising

In a release with AdMob enabled, Google's advertising SDK can process your IP address (including an approximate location inferred from it), advertising and other device identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses these for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The data and ad personalization depend on the SDK configuration, your choices and applicable rules.

This processing is described by Google's privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google describes SDK transmissions as encrypted in transit. Google and its providers may process data in countries other than yours, under the safeguards described in its policy. No additional advertising or mediation provider is configured in the current build.

Before enabling advertising that requires consent, SHIFT will provide the required privacy choices. Any required option to revisit those choices must also be available in the advertising release. You can also manage your advertising ID and available ad controls in Android settings. Choosing less personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all data processing needed to deliver or protect ads.

When you contact the developer

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include. This information is used to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. Correspondence is kept only as needed for these purposes and any applicable legal obligations; you may request its deletion by email.

Sharing, retention and security

The developer does not sell your support correspondence or saved game progress. Email services process correspondence to deliver and store messages. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Advertising data, when advertising is enabled, is processed by Google as explained above; this is separate from local puzzle progress.

Local game data remains until you reset it, remove app data or otherwise delete it, subject to Android backup settings. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data Google holds; see its retention explanation. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no device, storage or transmission method guarantees complete security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or transfer personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com to request help with information held by the developer. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully performed. For information held independently by Google, use the controls and contact methods in Google's privacy policy.

Children and families

The game does not ask players to submit their age or personal details. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact the email above so the request can be investigated and the information deleted when appropriate. Any advertising release must apply the age-related protections required for its audience and the countries where it is offered.

This policy page and changes

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Its handling is explained in the GitHub privacy statement. This page includes no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or external fonts.

The policy may change when SHIFT's features or data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will appear here; material changes will receive any additional notice or consent required by applicable rules.
